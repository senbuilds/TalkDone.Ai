# TalkDone.Ai

**Talk, Done!** Voice typing that cleans itself up.
Speak into any text box. TalkDone removes filler words and repetition, keeps your corrections, and types polished text where your cursor is.

**语音输入，自动整理。** 在任何输入框里开口说话，TalkDone 会去掉口头语和重复，保留你改口后的意思，把整理好的文字直接填进当前位置。

🌐 **Website / 官网：** https://talkdone.ai

## Why TalkDone / 为什么选 TalkDone

Voice typing today forces a trade-off. 现在的语音输入，用户只能二选一：

| | Closed-source cloud apps<br>闭源云端产品 | Open-source local apps<br>开源本地产品 | **TalkDone** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price 价格 | Expensive monthly subscriptions<br>月费贵，价格不由你控制 | Free<br>免费 | **A fraction of the price**<br>**同类的几分之一** |
| Your computer 本机负担 | Light<br>轻 | Heavy: large local models eat RAM/CPU and get slower over long sessions<br>本地大模型吃内存和 CPU，用久了越来越慢、越来越卡 | **Light: the heavy lifting runs in the cloud**<br>**轻：重活在云端** |
| Quality 质量 | High<br>高 | Depends on your hardware<br>取决于电脑配置 | **High: our own trained cleanup model**<br>**高：自研训练的整理模型** |

TalkDone runs on our own trained cleanup model, built to be efficient to serve. That efficiency is what lets us keep quality high while bringing the price way down.

TalkDone 用的是我们自己训练的整理模型，从一开始就为「高效运行」设计。正因为它省资源，我们才能在保证质量的同时，把价格降到很低。
