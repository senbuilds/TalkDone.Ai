# TalkDone.Ai

**English** | [简体中文](./README.zh-CN.md)

**Talk, Done!** Voice typing that cleans itself up.
Speak into any text box. TalkDone removes filler words and repetition, keeps your corrections, and types polished text where your cursor is.

🌐 **Website:** https://talkdone.ai

## Why TalkDone

Voice typing today forces a trade-off:

| | Closed-source cloud apps | Open-source local apps | **TalkDone** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | Expensive monthly subscriptions | Free | **A fraction of the price** |
| Your computer | Light | Heavy: large local models eat RAM and CPU, and get slower over long sessions | **Light: the heavy lifting runs in the cloud** |
| Quality | High | Depends on your hardware | **High: our own trained cleanup model** |

TalkDone runs on our own trained cleanup model, built to be efficient to serve. That efficiency is what lets us keep quality high while bringing the price way down.
